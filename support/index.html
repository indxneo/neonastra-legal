How to play

  • Orbit Dive: your orb circles a ring. Tap when it lines up with the arc of its own color on the next ring, and it dives in. Tap over empty space or the wrong color and the run ends.
  • Orbit Switch: tap to switch between two lanes and dodge the red hazards. Brushing past a hazard raises your multiplier, and stars add points.
  • Settings › How to Play explains every rule, including cascades, seams and the fast lane.

Continuing a run

  • After a game over you can tap Watch Ad to Continue to watch a short video ad and carry on from exactly where the run ended, after a 3-beat count-in.
  • You can continue once per run. A second game over ends the run.
  • The run continues only if you watch the ad to the end. If you close it early, or it can't be shown, the run ends as usual.
  • No button after a game over? No ad was available (for example, you're offline), you already continued once this run, or the run hadn't scored yet.

Sound and haptics

  • Silent mode mutes all game audio.
  • The music steps aside while other audio (your own music or a podcast) is playing.
  • Sound effects, music and haptics can be adjusted in Settings.

Language

Settings › Language: English, Español (México), Русский, or your iPhone's own language. It changes right away, with no restart.

Best scores and Game Center

  • Your best scores are kept on this iPhone. Settings › Reset Best Scores erases them.
  • If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores also appear on the leaderboards.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. In short: no accounts, an ad only when you choose to watch one, and we never receive your scores.
